About me

I'm a Senior Salesforce Consultant based in Melbourne, currently acting as functional lead at Monks (Mighty Hive), where I own system/solution design, configuration and release planning end to end. Before that I spent four years at Deloitte Digital, working my way from Salesforce Graduate to Senior Consultant across public sector, government and healthcare engagements.

My day-to-day is leading discovery, turning stakeholder requirements into solution-aligned process flows, and building the automation flows, approval processes, record types, page layouts that makes a Salesforce org hold up under real use. I also lead the solution design, coordinate release cycles across environments, and run backlog refinement using Agile delivery practices.
